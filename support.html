How to play

  • Tap your face on the title screen, then tap a game. You play against the cast, who take it easy on you.
  • The first time each game comes up, a card shows what wins and which buttons to tap. Pause, How to play brings it back.
  • One to four people can play on one screen with gamepads or keyboards. Bots fill any empty seat.
  • The game plays in landscape. Turn the device sideways.

Play online, everyone on their own phone

  • Tap your face, then Play online. One person taps Host a game and gets a four letter code.
  • Everyone else taps Join a game and types that code. Each person plays their own cast member on their own phone, in the same room or from different houses.
  • The host picks the game and taps START. The cast plays any empty seat. After each game everyone comes back to the same room for the next pick.

Common fixes

  • No sound: turn the volume up. The game plays even with the ring switch on silent.
  • Play online does not connect: every phone needs the internet. Check the four letter code with the host. It works only while the host's game is open.
  • The host left: the game ends for everyone. Join again with the new code.
  • A phone lost the internet: for a moment, it picks up where it left off. If it drops out for longer, the cast plays its part until the next game.
  • A gamepad does nothing: connect it before opening the game, then press a button on it.
  • Lost scores: scores live on the device, so deleting the app removes them.

Privacy

The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it collects nothing. It goes online only for Play online and Phone Controller. Read the privacy page.
