How to play

  • Everyone plays on one shared screen. Pick who you are on the title screen, then choose Quick Game vs Bots or set up a match.
  • One to four people can play. Bots fill any empty chair.
  • Touch works on the screen. Keyboards, mice and gamepads work too when the device has them.
  • The game plays in landscape. Turn the device sideways.

Common fixes

  • No sound: turn the volume up, and on an iPhone or iPad check that the silent switch is off.
  • A gamepad does nothing: connect it before opening the game, then press a button on it.
  • Lost scores: scores live on the device, so deleting the app removes them.

Privacy

The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it collects nothing. Read the privacy page.
