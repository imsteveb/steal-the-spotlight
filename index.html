Steal the Spotlight

A SKITS party game. Shell Knob In The Spotlight.

The game

Seven short party games on one theater set. One shared screen, one to four players, plus bots when a chair is empty. Play with touch, a keyboard, a mouse or a gamepad.

  • Steal the Spotlight
  • Musical Chairs
  • Peanut Gallery
  • Learn the Ropes
  • Shell Game
  • Kick Line
  • Prop Scramble

Nobody gets hurt. The only rough move is a hip check.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics, and nothing collected. Scores and settings stay on the device. Read the privacy page.
