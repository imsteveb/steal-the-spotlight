Steal the Spotlight

A SKITS party game. Shell Knob In The Spotlight.

This app does not have an account. It does not ask for your name, email, or location. Scores and control settings stay on the device. Nothing is sold. Nothing is sent to an ad network.

Phone controllers, if you use them, talk to the shared screen on your own Wi-Fi. That traffic does not leave the room.
