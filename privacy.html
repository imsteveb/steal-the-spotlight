Steal the Spotlight

A SKITS party game. Shell Knob In The Spotlight.

This app does not have an account. It does not ask for your name, email, or location. Scores and control settings stay on the device. Nothing is sold. Nothing is sent to an ad network.

Everything else happens on the device. If you never tap Play online or Phone Controller, the app never connects to the internet.

When you tap Play online (or Phone Controller, which turns a phone into a controller for a game on another screen), the app connects to our game server, a small relay that runs on Cloudflare. While you play, the phones in your game send each other what the game needs: which cast member each person picked, their moves and taps, and the game itself. The relay passes these along the moment they arrive and keeps nothing. No account, name, email or location is involved.

Like any internet connection, your device's internet address reaches the server while you are connected. We do not log it or keep it. A game's four letter code works only while that game is open.
